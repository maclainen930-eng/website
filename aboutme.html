Hi! I'm a videographer and photographer in the northern Utah area passionate about capturing the moments that matter most—especially in sports. What started as curiosity about my dad's professional camera when I was 13 has turned into my biggest creative outlet.

I got my own camera for my 14th birthday, and I've been hooked ever since. I started posting sports edits on Instagram (@mp4mac), and that's when I realized how much I loved telling stories through video and photography. But it wasn't until I discovered professional video editing software like DaVinci Resolve that everything clicked. Suddenly, I had access to tools that let me add visual effects, color grade my footage, and take my work to a whole new level of creativity.

Now, I focus on creating dynamic sports content that captures the energy, emotion, and intensity of the game. Whether it's highlight reels, game footage, or action shots, I bring a fresh perspective and attention to detail to every project. I understand what makes sports content compelling—the big plays, the hustle, the passion—and I know how to showcase it in a way that stands out.

If you're an athlete looking to build your highlight reel, or a parent wanting to preserve those unforgettable game moments or advertise your upcoming athlete to colleges, I'd love to work with you. Let's create something awesome together.
